FailLoud vs Guardrails AI

Short answer

Different layers. Guardrails AI validates content at call time — PII, toxicity, format and policy validators composed around an LLM call, with fix/reask actions. FailLoud verifies outcomes after the work: whether the actions an agent claims actually happened, backed by evidence. Guardrails screens what goes in and out; FailLoud audits what the agent says it did.

Choose Guardrails AI if…

your risk is in the content itself — PII leaking into a prompt, toxic or off-policy text coming out, malformed structured output — and you want validators wrapped around the call that can reask, fix or block in-process.

Choose FailLoud if…

your risk is an agent's report of the world: work claimed but not done, counts that don't reconcile, state that went stale. That audit happens on the output document, deterministically, and Guardrails AI does not attempt it.
